Alternate / practice S2

For S1=5–F: S2 0–7 selects TX/silent: 12/24, 20/20, 20/40, 20/100, 12/36, 16/48, 16/64, 60/0 seconds. S2 8–F repeats those cadences with a 60-minute startup delay. S2 C/D/E add 0–31 seconds of silent-phase jitter.

Field cautions

  1. Startup announcements may overlap during staggered setup; judge timing after all units are running.
  2. Independent crystal clocks drift. Check the order before participants begin.
  3. Use the intended operating frequency only when startup transmissions are acceptable.
